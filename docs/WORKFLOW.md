# Workflow

## Decision rule

Use Architect Apprentice when at least two are true:

1. More than one architecture is plausible.
2. A poor choice could create long-term debt or risk.
3. You want to learn the underlying engineering concept.
4. You expect to explain or maintain the decision later.

## Modes

### DESIGN_PRACTICE

You already know enough to sketch a solution.

```text
Problem frame
  ↓
Your first-pass architecture
  ↓
Socratic challenge
  ↓
Your revision
  ↓
Senior critique
  ↓
Structured handoff
```

### LEARN_FIRST

You need concepts before you can participate meaningfully.

```text
Problem frame
  ↓
Relevant concepts (no answer yet)
  ↓
Your first-pass architecture
  ↓
Socratic challenge
  ↓
Your revision
  ↓
Senior critique
  ↓
Structured handoff
```

## Production boundary

The learning workflow deliberately ends before implementation. For real software, pass the handoff into your professional delivery workflow.
